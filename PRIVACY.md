Phoca is a free and open-source graphical user interface for yt-dlp. This Privacy Policy outlines how the application handles your data.

1. Data Collection and Usage
Phoca does not collect, store, track, or transmit any personal data, telemetry, or analytics to the developer or any third parties.

3. Local Storage
Any settings, preferences, or download history are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted externally.

5. Network Connectivity
The application uses your internet connection strictly to interact with third-party media hosting platforms (via yt-dlp) to download the content you request. The developer of Phoca has no access to your search or download history.

7. Changes to this Policy
If any changes are made to how data is handled, this document will be updated accordingly.

9. Contact
If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
